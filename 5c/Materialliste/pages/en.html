Dear parents,

The following materials are needed for work in class. Of course, your child may continue to use the materials purchased during primary school.

Material list for lessons from Grade 5 onward (school year 2026/2027)

Pencil case with

One school fountain pen, including matching spare cartridges / two pencils (hardness: HB) / colored pencils (at least 12 colors) / eraser (blue/red) / sharpener
2 highlighters, yellow, pink / one set square / one glue stick / craft scissors (rounded tips, fitting into the pencil case);

8 exercise books DIN-A4, ruling No. 25 (lined, wide margin)

3 exercise books DIN-A4, ruling No. 26 (squared, wide margin)

one exercise book, ruling No. 20 (unlined)

one notebook, DIN A5, squared,

1 transparent folder DIN-A4 for parent information

Collage pad DIN A 4, with squared and lined paper,
Drawing pad DIN A 3, pure white (fixed on both sides) (art)

one plastic box (e.g. available cheaply at Action or Tedi), with Pelikan paint box 12 colors, opaque white, one brush set, one cloth and one cup

one folder for A3 (art)

one drinking bottle for refilling,

one pair of gym shoes that provide real support (no sneakers), T-shirt or polo shirt, one badminton racket + balls (at least 3)

We also recommend using a neck pouch to store money and ID cards. The school cannot accept liability for valuables.

The exam exercise books will be purchased by the school; the contribution toward the cost should be paid to the class teacher during the first weeks of school.

Dear parents, the list of required work materials is carefully aligned with lessons at our school. Please understand if subject teachers ask you to purchase additional items. You should therefore wait before buying a compass and a dictionary until the teachers inform you about this. Please also take note of “Every child can have a book” on the next page.

In class, we try to bring environmentally conscious thinking and action closer to your child. Please also pay attention to environmentally friendly materials when shopping.

“Every child can have a book”

At our school, we would like to encourage and preserve the joy of reading. Therefore, we ask every child to carry a book of their own choice in their school bag every day.

The book can be a novel, a non-fiction book, a comic or another age-appropriate reading text - what matters is that your child enjoys reading it.

The book will be used regularly during short reading times in lessons, in our reading corner or in other quiet work phases. This allows us to use idle times meaningfully for reading and at the same time promote reading competence.

Please therefore make sure that your child has a book with them whenever possible. The book may of course also be borrowed from the city library.

During this school year, our school media library will also become available, so your child will be able to borrow books there as well.

We thank you for your understanding and your support and send kind regards:
